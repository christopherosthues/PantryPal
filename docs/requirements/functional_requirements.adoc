= Functional Requirements: PantryPal
:toc: left

== Food Management
*   **FR-1.1: List Food Items:** The system shall display a list of all food items associated with the current profile.
*   **FR-1.2: Search:** Users shall be able to search the food list by name.
*   **FR-1.3: Sort:** The system shall support sorting by name and expiration date (ascending/descending).
*   **FR-1.4: Filter:** Users shall be able to filter items by "Opened" status, "Unopened" status and "Overdue" status.
*   **FR-1.5: Detailed View:** The system shall show detailed nutritional info: Calories, Joule, Fat, Saturated Fat, Carbs, Sugar, Fiber, Protein, and Salt.
*   **FR-1.6: Expiration Warning:** The system shall provide visual warnings for items past their Best-before date (MHD) and stronger warnings for items past their Use-by date (VD).
*   **FR-1.7: Price Tracking:** The system shall allow users to record the purchase price of a food item or an inventory item.
*   **FR-1.8: Price History:** The system shall automatically track changes to the price of a product over time, creating a history of price points (date and price) linked to the inventory item.
*   **FR-1.9: Price Visualization:** The system shall provide a visual representation (e.g., a chart) of the price history for a specific product.

== Inventory Management
*   **FR-2.1: Frequently used Items:** The system shall provide a separate inventory list for frequently used items so that new food items can be created quickly.
*   **FR-2.2: Multi-Image Support:** Users shall be able to attach a main image and multiple additional images to an inventory item.

== Smart Data Entry (OCR)
*   **FR-3.1: OCR Scan:** The system shall use the device camera to scan and recognize text from product labels.
*   **FR-3.2: Automated Mapping:** The system shall automatically map recognized text to fields: Name, Quantity, Expiration Date, and Nutritional Table.

== User Accounts & Sync
*   **FR-4.1: Authentication:** Users shall be able to register and log in to a remote server.
*   **FR-4.2: Sync Modes:** Users shall be able to choose between:
    *   No Sync (Local only)
    *   Only Upload (Backup)
    *   Only Download (Restore)
    *   Bidirectional Sync
*   **FR-4.3: Conflict Resolution:** The system shall use "Last Modified" timestamps to resolve data conflicts during sync.
