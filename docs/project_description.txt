PantryPal is a cross-platform app for the efficient management of food items, their nutritional values, and expiration dates.

1. Core Functions of Food Management
The user has a central overview of all managed food items.
- Search & Filtering: The list can be searched by name. Filters allow for narrowing down to opened/unopened products as well as the expiration status (e.g., "Overdue").
- Sorting: Sorting by name and expiration date (ascending and descending) is integrated.
- Status Visualization:
    * Opened food items are marked.
    * Products that have exceeded their best-before date (BBD) are highlighted with a warning icon.
    * Products that have exceeded their use-by date are also highlighted with a color/icon, but with an even more distinct warning, as health risks exist here.
- Detailed View: In the detailed view, name, quantity (weight/volume), opening date, and specific nutritional values (calories, fat, saturated fatty acids, carbohydrates, sugar, dietary fiber, protein, salt) can be maintained.

2. Inventory Management
In addition to short-lived food management, the app offers an inventory list for long-lived goods or supplies without a specific expiration date.
- Collection of master data and nutritional values for frequently used items.
- Management of primary images and additional product photos for better identification.

3. Smart Data Capture (OCR & Camera)
To simplify data entry, PantryPal supports camera-assisted capture:
- OCR Recognition: Automatic extraction of product names, quantities, expiration dates, and complete nutritional tables from the packaging.
- Image Support: Multiple images (primary image and gallery) can be assigned to each entry, which are stored locally and optionally synchronized.

4. Cloud Synchronization & User Accounts
PantryPal supports both local offline operation and server-based storage.
- User Profiles: Users can create profiles to use their data across multiple devices.
- Synchronization Modes: In the settings, you can choose between:
    * Local storage only (No synchronization)
    * Upload only (Backup of local data)
    * Download only (Sync from server)
    * Bidirectional synchronization (Upload and download)
- Conflict Management: Timestamps (Last Modified) ensure that the most current version of an entry is always kept during synchronization.

5. Technical Platform & Design
- Multi-platform Support: The app is developed for Android, Desktop (JVM), and other platforms (Kotlin Multiplatform).
- Personalization: Support for light and dark mode (theme management) as well as dynamic adaptation to system settings.
- Data Security: Local storage in an encrypted/structured database (SQL) and secure authentication on the server.
